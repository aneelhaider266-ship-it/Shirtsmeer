## What Color Pants Goes with a Grey Shirt?

If you start with a grey shirt, the answer is simple. Navy, charcoal, black and white pants all work. Choose pants that are clearly lighter or darker than the shirt. Keep the shoes dark and plain. The same rule helps when you ask what color pants with grey shirt looks best, because contrast does most of the work.

## Understanding Tonal Values: Charcoal vs. Mid-Grey vs. Light Grey

Before you pick a shirt, check where your grey pants sit on the greyscale. A shirt that looks great with light dove grey chinos can look flat with dark charcoal wool.

### Light Grey and Silver Trousers

Light grey trousers reflect more light, so they look lighter. They give deep shirt colors a clean backdrop. A dark navy, forest green or black shirt stands out well against them.

### Mid-Grey and Slate Trousers

Mid-grey is the workhorse of the business wardrobe. It has enough depth for pale shirts, but it is light enough to contrast with darker ones. If you are building a small wardrobe, buy mid-grey trousers first.

### Dark Grey and Charcoal Trousers

Charcoal trousers look almost like black pants in modern tailoring. Because of this, charcoal needs light shirts, such as pure white, ice blue or pale ecru. A dark shirt can look murky with charcoal unless you add a jacket or strong accessories.

## Can You Wear a Grey Shirt with Grey Pants?

Yes, but only on purpose. The shirt and pants must be clearly different shades. A dark grey shirt looks sharp with light grey pants, and a light grey shirt looks clean with dark grey pants. If the two greys are almost the same, the outfit looks like a failed match.

Follow these rules when you try it:

* Keep at least two shades of difference between the shirt and the pants.
* Add a third color, such as a navy blazer or brown shoes, to break up the grey.
* Mix textures, such as a smooth cotton shirt with flannel trousers.

For a smart casual look, grey shirt matching pants combinations work well when the shades are clearly different. Grey is also the easiest neutral for beginners, because white, light blue and pink shirts all work with it.

## Common Styling Mistakes to Avoid with Grey Pants

* Nearly identical greys: do not wear a grey shirt that is almost the same shade as your pants.
* Loud jewel tones: bright yellow, electric green and bright purple clash with grey.
* Mismatched leather: a black belt with brown shoes ruins the look.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What color pants go with a grey shirt?** Navy, charcoal, black and white pants all go with a grey shirt. Dark grey pants work with a light grey shirt, and light grey pants work with a dark grey shirt.

**What color shirts go with grey dress pants?** White, light blue, pale pink and navy shirts go well with grey dress pants. Pick a light shirt for charcoal pants and a darker shirt for light grey pants.

**What color shirt goes best with grey pants?** White and light blue are the safest choices with grey pants. They give clean contrast on charcoal and mid-grey trousers and work for almost every dress code.

**Can I wear a black shirt with grey pants?** Yes, but contrast matters. A black shirt looks best with light grey or dove grey pants. With dark charcoal pants, it can look muddy unless you add a contrasting jacket.

**What color tie goes with grey pants?** Navy, burgundy and forest green ties look excellent with grey pants. A silver or light grey tie also works if the pants are a darker shade.

**What color shoes go with grey pants?** Black, dark brown and burgundy leather shoes all work. Black is the most formal, dark brown is warmer, and burgundy adds depth to mid-grey flannel.

**Can you wear a brown belt with grey pants?** Yes, as long as the belt matches your shoes. A brown belt with brown shoes looks polished, but mixing black and brown leather does not.
